## Completion write-up (2026-07-17)

**Done:** Barracks and Archery Range are now locked to producing one chosen unit type at a time - `TrainingGround.chosen_unit_type` (`scripts/training_ground.gd`), defaulted per building via `BuildingCatalog`'s new `"chosen_unit_type"` field, changeable any time (free, instant) through a new "Train: \<Unit\>" option added to that building's existing Recruit/Upgrade panel. `Base._on_attack_pressed` (`scripts/base.gd`) reads it directly on deployment now - the old global round-robin (`MELEE_ARCHETYPES`, which picked a citizen's concrete unit type by squad-roster position rather than any real choice) is deleted.

Two new `CombatUnit.UnitType`s: **Pikeman** (Barracks, replaces Trapper's slot - a spear reach `attack_range` of 65 vs the standard melee 45, and a 2.0x damage bonus against Outrider specifically, its "hard counter vs cavalry" per this note) and **Skirmisher** (Archery Range's alternative to Archer - less range/damage/HP but faster attacks and movement, plus a 1.8x damage bonus against Archer specifically, the "archer counter" per this note). Full stats/multipliers in `Outpost_Survival/Game Systems/Balance.md`'s Combat tables.

**Scope decided with the user before implementing:** the Stable (Outrider/Chevalier/Cavalry Archer) is deferred entirely, not part of this pass. Trapper is retired the same way, for the same reason (replaced at Barracks, not deleted) - both remain fully functional and testable via the free-play combat sandbox.
