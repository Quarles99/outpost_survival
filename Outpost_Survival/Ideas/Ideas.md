# Ideas

Freeform design ideas and concepts for Outpost Survival — one note per idea, linked to related ideas and to the [[Journal]] entries where they came up. Also the implementation backlog (see `CLAUDE.md`).

Not authoritative — an idea only becomes real once it's built and documented in `Docs/` (`Outpost_Survival/Docs/`). Once an idea is fully implemented (completion write-up added, per `CLAUDE.md`'s convention), move its note into `Completed/` so this folder stays a clean, current backlog rather than a mix of pending and finished work.
