# Planning

Active work-in-progress tracking, separate from [[Journal]] (retrospective session notes) and [[Ideas]] (not-yet-started backlog).

One note per multi-session or multi-step effort currently in flight: current phase/status, what's done, what's blocked or deferred, and links to the Journal entries and Ideas notes it came from. A plan approved via Claude Code's plan mode gets summarized here too (not just left in `~/.claude/plans/`), so its status is visible from the vault without needing access to that external location.

Once an effort is fully done, fold a closing summary into the relevant `Docs/` page (per `CLAUDE.md`'s standing documentation-maintenance rule) and either delete this note or mark it closed at the top - this folder is for things still moving, not a permanent archive.
